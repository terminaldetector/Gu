Gu changes: the official Traced x Traced aggregated connection graph is
converted into sorted FLY1 CSR, retaining all 165,122 Traced neuronal bodies
(including isolated bodies) across brain, optic lobes and ventral nerve cord.
Non-Traced fragments and glia are not artificial computational neurons.
No additional synapse-count threshold is applied. Anatomical pair weights
are converted into signed LIF currents using a static 0.275 scale and the
official consensus_nt property: acetylcholine positive; GABA, glutamate and
histamine negative; unknown or other/modulatory transmitters zero.
The zero-weight connections remain in the anatomical graph. Receptor-specific
and neuromodulatory dynamics are not represented by this approximation.
See male-cns.fly.json in the APK for exact source fingerprints, sign policy,
graph fingerprint, counts and experimental retinal/motor port mappings.
The data creators do not endorse Gu or its simulations.
