Bundled dataset: Shiu Drosophila brain model, FlyWire v783.
Source: https://github.com/philshiu/Drosophila_brain_model
FlyWire data have separate applicable dataset terms; see https://zenodo.org/records/10676866.

Bundled emulator engines have their own licenses:
- JSNES: MIT; lab/JSNES-LICENSE.txt.
- Genesis Plus GX: noncommercial license; lab/sega/LICENSE.txt and
  lab/sega/genplus-source.zip contain the applicable terms and full source.
- Gambatte (GB/GBC): GPL-2.0-or-later; lab/gb/source.zip contains the
  upstream license and complete corresponding source at the revision in
  lab/gb/source.json.
- Snes9x (SNES/SFC): Snes9x noncommercial license; lab/snes/source.zip
  contains the applicable terms and complete corresponding source at the
  revision in lab/snes/source.json.
The FlyConsole libretro frontend is MIT; its full license is bundled as
lab/gb/FLY-LICENSE.txt and lab/snes/FLY-LICENSE.txt and in each source archive.
The bundled diagnostic cartridges are original FlyConsole test programs.
Commercial game ROMs are not bundled.
